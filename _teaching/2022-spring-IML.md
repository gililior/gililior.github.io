For three years (2021-2024), I taught 2 groups of BSc students (~80 students) the basics of machine learning.
Through teaching, I deepened my knowledge and understanding of ML basics, as well as my instructing skills.
Alongside a few other TAs, I was in charge of the students' homework and the course logistics, and I held office hours to help students who needed assistance with the course material.
